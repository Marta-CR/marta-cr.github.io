## Join us

We welcome enquiries from motivated researchers interested in computational chemistry, astrochemistry, and the mechanisms of organic and organometallic reactions. Our research explores how complex organic molecules form in the interstellar medium and how molecular structure and reaction pathways govern reactivity and enantioselectivity.

If you are interested in pursuing a PhD or postdoctoral research with us, please contact **Dr Marta Castiñeira Reis** directly to discuss your interests and potential opportunities.

### Prospective PhD students

Please send a CV including the grades obtained during your bachelor's and master's studies, together with a motivation letter outlining your research interests, relevant experience, and reasons for wishing to join us.

### Prospective postdoctoral researchers

Please send a detailed CV, including your publication list, and a motivation letter describing your research experience, scientific interests, and how these relate to our research lines.

Applications will only be considered once all the requested documents have been received.

### Fellowship applications

We encourage prospective postdoctoral researchers to explore independent fellowship funding. We will support selected candidates whose interests align with our research in developing their applications, including defining a suitable project and preparing the research proposal. Please contact us well in advance of the relevant deadline.

Potential funding opportunities include:

- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships).
- [Human Frontier Science Program (HFSP) Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.hfsp.org/funding/hfsp-funding/postdoctoral-fellowships), for interdisciplinary projects addressing fundamental questions in the life sciences.

The suitability of each scheme will depend on the proposed project and the candidate's eligibility.
