Academic appointments, research, teaching and service.

Marta Castiñeira Reis

Computational chemist · Lecturer, University of Oviedo

ORCID · Publications

I study chemical reaction mechanisms using theoretical and computational chemistry. My research connects stereoselective and organometallic catalysis with astrochemistry, focusing on how complex organic molecules can form in the interstellar medium. My work combines electronic-structure calculations, reaction-pathway exploration, kinetics and high-performance computing.

Research interests

  • Astrochemistry and the formation of complex organic molecules in the interstellar medium.
  • Automated reaction discovery, reaction networks and chemical kinetics.
  • Stereoselective catalysis and organic and organometallic reaction mechanisms.
  • Electronic-structure methods, reaction dynamics and molecular modelling.
  • High-performance computing for chemical research.
